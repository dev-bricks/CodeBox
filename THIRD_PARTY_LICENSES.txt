## Release Notes & Compatibility

- **MIT Compatibility**: CodeBox is distributed under the MIT license.
  All third-party libraries and runtime components are dynamically linked or
  invoked via subprocess (LSP), satisfying LGPL-3.0-only, LGPL-2.1-or-later,
  and permissive (MIT/BSD/Apache-2.0) requirements.
- Standalone packaging using PyInstaller is covered by the official
  Bootloader-exception, which allows non-GPL binary distribution.
- Preserve upstream license files and copyright notices bundled inside installed wheels
  and source distributions.
- Re-run this inventory when `requirements.txt` or `pyproject.toml` dependencies change.
